The prior RAG system relied on hard-coded legal rules, reducing flexibility and increasing maintenance. By building a knowledge-graph-based retrieval system using a domain ontology and RDB-to-RDF mappings, we replaced these rules, achieving comparable performance through graph-based retrieval and embeddings.
