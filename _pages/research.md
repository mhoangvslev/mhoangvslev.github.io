<h2 class="research-title">LLMs and Knowledge Graphs</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Grounding LLMs in knowledge graphs (GraphRAG) to reduce hallucination, and coupling generative models with linked data for natural-language interaction, including a federated query builder with an end-user interface. Earlier work enriched prompts with Wikidata to improve BERT/RoBERTa question answering.</p>

<h2 class="research-title">SPARQL Federation and Query Processing</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Scalable querying of many knowledge graphs at once: the FedShop benchmark, the FedUP federation engine with result-aware plans, RAW-JENA for approximate query processing, and join-order optimisation for SPARQL property path queries.</p>

<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Schema.org and the Web as a Knowledge Graph</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Can LLMs be trusted to extract facts from the Web? LLM4Schema.org evaluates generated Schema.org markup with deterministic and LLM-as-judge metrics, and a characteristic-set observatory shows how Schema.org is actually used.</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="research-body">
<h2 class="research-title">Enterprise Knowledge Graphs and Retrieval</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Domain ontologies and RDB-to-RDF mappings to replace hard-coded retrieval rules in a legal RAG system, plus synthetic query generation to fine-tune long-context embedding models (a 30 percentage-point recall gain for an Agentic RAG system).</p>
