I am a postdoctoral researcher at Inria in the Wimmics team, working on natural language based secure federated query building: grounding LLMs in Knowledge Graphs (GraphRAG) to reduce hallucination, and coupling generative models with linked data. I previously worked as an applied research and development engineer at JusMundi, and completed my PhD at Nantes University (LS2N) on querying the Web as a Knowledge Graph.

My research interests: Semantic Web, Knowledge / Ontology Engineering, Linked Data, Enterprise Knowledge Graphs, Artificial Intelligence, and Music Information Retrieval.

Feel free to reach out about collaborations, research opportunities, or Semantic Web and LLM projects!
